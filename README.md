# MYNUTRITION — Fuel Your Body. Build Your Best.

A modern, aesthetic, and high-converting ecommerce web experience for **MYNUTRITION**, a new-generation protein nutrition brand designed for **Gen Z and active young adults (18–35)**.

---

## 🌟 Live Demo & Pages

- **Homepage (`index.html`)**: Complete high-converting ecommerce landing page featuring Hero, "Why MYNUTRITION" 4 pillars, interactive Featured Product container mockup with dynamic flavor switcher (Chocolate, Vanilla, Coffee), "Why Protein Matters", "How It Fits Your Day" routines, Customer Reviews, FAQ accordion, slide-over Cart Drawer, and full footer.
- **About Us (`about.html`)**: Editorial brand story detailing *Our Story*, *Our Mission*, *The 6 Brand Pillars*, *Our Promise* (clean ingredients, independent lab testing, GMP certifications), and *Built for Your Everyday* lifestyle moments.
- **Design System (`design-system/MASTER.md`)**: Full 60-30-10 palette, Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter typography scale, and 8pt grid tokens.

---

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic Highlights

- **60-30-10 Color System**:
  - `60%` Off-White Cream (`#FAF8F5`)
  - `30%` Deep Forest Sage (`#2C4232`) & Warm Beige (`#F4EFEA`)
  - `10%` Solar Terracotta Accent (`#D96B43`)
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings) + Inter (Body).
- **Anti-Cliche**: Strictly free from aggressive "gym-bro" neon skulls; tailored for everyday wellness, students, beginners, and active lifestyles.
- **Zero Build Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, Tailwind CSS (via CDN), and Lucide vector icons.

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages

1. In your repository on GitHub, go to **Settings** (⚙️).
2. On the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: **main** (or master)
   - Folder: `/ (root)`
4. Click **Save**.
5. Your website will be live in ~60 seconds at:
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`
